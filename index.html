FAQ

Questions owners actually ask

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use this?
No. Everyone logs in with a mobile number. Field staff use it from a phone between visits; front desk logs visits in under 30 seconds. If your team can use WhatsApp, they can use FlowCare.
Is this only for physiotherapy?
No. FlowCare is built for any care or wellness practice that serves clients both at home and on-site — home nursing, personal training, therapy, dietetics, diagnostic sample collection, and more. If your staff sometimes travel to the client and pay includes commission, FlowCare fits.
What happens to my existing client records?
We help you bring existing records in during onboarding — clients, staff, and fee structures. You don't start from a blank page.
Can I manage more than one location?
Yes. Run multiple locations from one owner login. Every client, visit, and staff record stays cleanly separated per location — with roll-up reports when you want the whole picture.
How is staff commission calculated?
You set a commission percentage per person once. The system applies it to what each field practitioner collects on home visits, adds base pay, and generates monthly payroll in one click. Staff can see exactly how their number was calculated.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Password-protected accounts, encrypted login, role-based permissions so staff only see what they need, and an activity log for every action taken in the system.
